# Curating Melbourne morning places

This is a **small, checked collection**, not a live POI feed. The `source` URL in `places.ts` is the venue's own information page. Map points were looked up once through OpenStreetMap Nominatim; no user search requests go to Nominatim. The map uses OpenFreeMap vector tiles, and its attribution stays visible. Do not put Google Places results on this non-Google map.

## Add or refresh a place

1. Start with a named origin and walking radius, not remembered landmarks. Browse an official local directory (City of Melbourne places/What's On, Museums Victoria, council libraries), then search relevant OSM categories and the venue's own site. A failed Overpass request is an endpoint failure, not evidence of no places. Check for duplicate parent/child venues: a museum's exhibition, a garden's Conservatory, and a ticketed tour do not inherit the parent's hours.
2. Verify the exact venue and address on its own site. Record its ordinary weekly schedule **and** the current date's holiday, closure, install, session or sell-out exception. If the date is unverified, use `uncertainDates` or `hours: null`; do not invent a closing time from “till late.” An explicit dated closure belongs in `exceptions` as `null`. Recheck date-specific overrides before future holidays. `availability()` says “listed” rather than claiming someone is physically open right now.
   Use the [Victorian public-holiday calendar](https://business.vic.gov.au/business-information/public-holidays/victorian-public-holidays-2026) to trigger venue-specific checks; the holiday alone does not prove closure. State Library Victoria's “most public holidays” wording does not establish 25 September's hours, so that date is marked uncertain.
3. Geocode the *exact address* using Nominatim at no more than one request per second; inspect its OSM object, geometry and official address. A node near a door is preferable to a building or park centroid. For a tour meeting at a different location, pin the meeting point or omit it. Do not use Nominatim as an autocomplete service from each visitor's browser. See [Nominatim policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/).
4. Write a short description and practical tip supported by the source; keep prices, booking and accessibility caveats where they change the decision. Do not promise the person can finish a visit in a short window. Walking minutes are a straight-line-derived estimate, not pedestrian routing.
5. Check the candidate in the browser with the chosen origin and a real short time window. Test a closed day/holiday, an arrival just before closing, a changed timezone or DST boundary, and a session-only/unknown place. Verify the external venue link and Google Maps destination use the intended location.

## Evidence for the latest Carlton expansion

Checked 25 September 2026. Coordinates are longitude, latitude; OSM objects identify the mapped point, **not** a verified pedestrian entrance.

- **Brunswick Street Gallery** — [venue hours and install-period warning](https://brunswickstreetgallery.com.au/pages/contact); [OSM node 1228815180](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/1228815180) at 144.978782, −37.7976732. Public-holiday operation unconfirmed.
- **Heartattack and Vine** — [venue hours, “8am till late,” walk-in policy](https://www.heartattackandvine.com.au/); [OSM node 331166210](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/331166210) at 144.9672504, −37.7976471. No precise closing or holiday hours; marked unknown.
- **Kathleen Syme Library** — [council's branch hours and Victorian-public-holiday closure](https://www.melbourne.vic.gov.au/library-locations-and-opening-hours); [OSM way 383402005](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/383402005) centroid at 144.9652521, −37.7987135. Closed 25 September; do not use community-centre room hours as library hours.
- **Melbourne City Baths** — [council hours and published holiday table](https://www.melbourne.vic.gov.au/melbourne-city-baths); [OSM way 26564416](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/26564416) centroid at 144.963295, −37.8071077. The holiday table omits 25 September; unknown that day. Pools close before building hours.
- **Chinese Museum** — [venue daily 10am–4pm and public-holiday closure](https://www.chinesemuseum.com.au/location-hours); [OSM way 46197639](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/46197639) centroid at 144.969209, −37.8107366. Closed 25 September.
- **Fitzroy Gardens** — [council outdoor garden and walk](https://www.melbourne.vic.gov.au/fitzroy-gardens); [OSM way 4817020](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/4817020) park centroid at 144.9805054, −37.8126840. Garden access hours unverified. The separate [Conservatory is closed for display changes 21–25 September](https://whatson.melbourne.vic.gov.au/things-to-do/the-conservatory); it is not listed as open.

The original ten venues' coordinates were also one-off Nominatim lookups, but their OSM object IDs were not retained. Reconcile those before claiming entrance-level accuracy. `places.ts` retains official venue links; it does not refresh them automatically. This collection remains concentrated in inner Melbourne. For another city, a new curated collection or an explicitly licensed POI service is needed; moving the map alone will not discover new places.
